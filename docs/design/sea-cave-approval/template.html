WestCose Sea Caves

The hidden beach becomes a rock headland with a cave system inside it, entered from the public beach through a rebuilt tunnel beneath the lighthouse. The lighthouse doubles in height and gets an outside stair to a balcony. Every decision below was approved as drawn. These drawings are rendered from the data the world is built from.

Decisions

All five approved as drawn on 2026-10-02

Sheet 1. Plan

North is up. Contours every 1.5 m. The dark shape is the cave, drawn at its rock face; the walkable floor stops 0.35 m inside it so nobody's shoulder clips a wall. Pillars are the gaps inside it. The red bar on the east cliff is the Ocean Window, and the dashed circle in the Grotto is the clear arena kept for the future game.

The cliff stays inside the terrain the world already draws (red dashed line). The rock mass stops short of the Cliff Cul-de-sac streets and the lighthouse trail.

Sheet 2. Long section

True scale, no vertical exaggeration

Follows the main route: beach mouth, tunnel under the lighthouse, Undercroft, the Crawl, the Grotto, then up the ramp onto the window ledge. The floor never drops below sea level, and the ledge lip is 3.3 m above the water with a boulder lip. Walkers in the cave can't step past the lip: outside the cave plan the ground counts as solid rock, so the drop to the sea is unreachable.

Sheet 3. Cross sections

Lines A, B and C are marked on the plan

A shows the rebuilt tunnel with about 2 m of rock between its roof and the lighthouse terrace. B cuts across both routes to the Grotto: the Crawl is wide and low (about 3 m), the Slot narrow (1.7–2.4 m) and tall (up to 8 m). C runs north through the Grotto dome, the pillar between the Twin Arches, and the Tide Pool.

Interpretation limits

Terrain is sampled on a 0.5 m grid from the live world modules; the rendered cave mesh will add chunky rock facets and formations that are not drawn here. North of the rock mass the old cove is shown as it is today; with approval its sand becomes low rocky shoreline (decision 5).

Rev B, after approval: the window ledge was squared to the cliff face, its ramp lengthened to stay under 35°, the arena disc set at 10 m across, and the town terrain extended 3.6 m east so the cliffs sit inside it. Sizes and decisions are unchanged.
