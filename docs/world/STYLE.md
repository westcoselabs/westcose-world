# WestCose World — active style direction

WestCose World is a playable spherical portfolio set in a fictional California coastal town. “Dead Coast” is the historical name used in earlier iteration documents, not the current user-facing name. The original palette and material guide remains [WESTCOSE_WORLD_STYLE.md](../WESTCOSE_WORLD_STYLE.md); the current [coastal layout](COASTAL_LAYOUT.md) supersedes earlier shoreline, pier and building-placement details. Preserve the current spherical runtime.

Use faded California coastal architecture, converted workshops, print studios, independent storefronts, residential infill and a working waterfront. Author building clusters, corners and interrupted sightlines. Avoid a resort, cyberpunk city, identical procedural houses or empty terrain.

Keep a readable sequence from town to ocean: dry storefront setbacks, a continuous promenade, broad sand and a narrow raised timber pier with visible piles and a small fishing head. The Arcade belongs on land. Coastal oak and windswept cypress groves carry detail around the rest of the planet, with open trail corridors, scrub and rocky lookouts connecting the quieter headlands to the denser town.

Palette: bone #E9DFCE; concrete #C5BFB2; sand #CEB586; wet sand #AD9775; graphite #242C2E; steel #647778; blue #5B7E8A; shallow water #649497; deep water #345E6A; foam #EBE8D9; rust #A66A45; warm light #E5B574; sky #C8D7D5.

Materials are worn and legible in low coastal sunset light. Detail comes from silhouettes, facade layers, original fictional signage, utilities and composed clutter. No approved WestCose logo art was supplied; environmental WestCose signs use plain text, not an invented replacement logo.

The latest sunset pass strengthens the supplied rain-courtyard direction: low amber sunlight, steel-blue shade, a muted peach horizon, and warm entrance lights. This supersedes the earlier bright-daylight preference. Preserve readable walking routes and interiors while allowing deeper shade and a stronger contrast between sunlight and shelter. Mineral plaster has concentrated salt staining and runoff; stone paving has varied warm/cool bodies, chipped joints, damp patches and repairs. Iron drains, soil beds and restrained edge debris ground the architecture. Trees have branching windswept silhouettes, with denser small leaves, ferns, dune grasses and selected wall ivy. Planting belongs in authored pockets and trellises, leaving entrances and route widths open.

Use shared physically based materials and subtle bump/roughness variation. Texture relief is visual only; it never modifies walking support. Keep water inexpensive. The current damp-surface reflections use a small generated sky environment, not realtime reflected copies of the town. No photographic asset pack or external texture service is required by this pass.

Interiors remain in the world with physically open doorways and simple collision. Their exhibits reference the content registry; substantial copy stays in HTML. The world UI stays compact and optional. A small field-note panel and planet overview support exploration without a persistent directory overlay.
